# English Islands — Tamires

Personalized 10-island curriculum based on the learner profile and goals.

## Goal
Reach functional conversational fluency, especially the ability to hold a long Discord conversation without translating every sentence.

## Islands
1. Who I Am — personal introduction and identity
2. My Everyday Life — routine and daily situations
3. Friends & Discord — conversation repair and social interaction
4. Stories & Experiences — past events and storytelling
5. Travel & Culture — travel survival and cultural conversation
6. Mind & Relationships — feelings, friendships and behavior
7. Ideas & Philosophy — opinions, arguments and abstract topics
8. Technology & AI — technology, AI, programming and projects
9. Work & Career — quality, construction, renewable energy and interviews
10. Real English — natural speech, reductions and spontaneous conversation

Each island contains contextual vocabulary, core sentences with Portuguese support, patterns, questions with sample answers, translation/fill/rearrange/multiple-choice/listening exercises, speaking practice, shadowing, talk prompts and a final challenge.

The content is intentionally centered on the learner's real life and interests rather than generic textbook topics.
